## 📈 Amazon Stock Market Analysis Dashboard (Excel)
## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Excel dashboard developed to analyze Amazon stock price behavior and trading volume over multiple years. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of price movements, volume trends, and time-based patterns to support financial and investment analysis.
The solution leverages advanced Excel features, including Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, slicers, and calculated metrics, to transform raw stock data into meaningful insights.

## About Dataset
- 10 columns 
- 3,949 rows 
- <a href="https://github.com/SANJNAKHATRI/Amazon-Stock-Market-Analysis-Dashboard/blob/main/Amazon%20Dataaset.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Project Objectives
- Analyze open, close, high, and low stock prices 
- Identify daily, monthly, and yearly price trends 
- Evaluate trading volume behavior over time
- Compare open vs close prices by weekday
- Enable interactive filtering by day, month, and year

## Key Business Questions
- What is the average open, close, high, and low price of Amazon stock?
- How does trading volume vary by month and weekday?
- Which days show higher volatility (high vs low spread)?
- What are the monthly trends in adjusted close prices?
- How has Amazon’s stock price evolved year over year?

## Key Metrics & KPIs
- Average Open Price
- Average Close Price
- Average High Price
- Average Low Price
- Trading Volume by Time
- Monthly Average Adjusted Close Price
- Yearly Trend in Adjusted Close

## Analysis Process

1 Data Preparation

   - Imported historical Amazon stock data into Excel

2 Pivot Table & Chart Creation

   - Created Pivot Tables for price and volume aggregation

   - Built dynamic Pivot Charts for trend visualization

3 Dashboard Development

   - Designed an interactive dashboard layout

   - Implemented slicers for weekday, month, and year filtering

4 Insights Generation

   - Analyzed price volatility, seasonal patterns, and long-term trends
